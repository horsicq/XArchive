Focused libarchive-derived native-reader notice
================================================

XFileUnpacker does not build or link libarchive.  Native XArchive code for
WARC, mtree, UU transport decoding, and the AFIO large-ASCII CPIO subtype was
implemented from the corresponding reader behavior in libarchive 3.8.9:
